The Muslim Platform

Islamic web platform & recitation engine

Open live demo ↗ Lead Full Stack Developer · 2024
01 — The problem

People need prayer times, Qibla direction, Quran recitations and daily Athkar in one place, and the page has to keep working when the connection drops.

02 — The solution

A Next.js 14 app with a service worker for offline use. Prayer times come from Adhan.js, the Qibla compass uses the device orientation, and the Athkar counter keeps its state between visits.

03 — What I built
  • —Full Quran with audio recitations
  • —Prayer times calculated with Adhan.js
  • —Interactive Qibla compass
  • —Athkar counter with saved state
  • —Offline support through a PWA service worker
  • —Web Push notifications
04 — Challenges & fixes
CHALLENGE

The app's whole purpose is serving you on the go — prayer times, recitation and the counter are useless the second the connection drops.

FIX

A service worker with an offline-first caching strategy, so the complete feature set keeps working with zero connection.

CHALLENGE

Quran audio, prayer times, a Qibla compass and an Athkar counter had to live in one single page — loading it all eagerly made the app crawl and drain the battery.

FIX

Heavy features mount only on their own tabs, audio streams on demand, and React.memo keeps re-renders out of the hot path.

CHALLENGE

Prayer times and the Qibla direction must be accurate at runtime, and recomputing astronomy math on every state change wastes CPU.

FIX

Adhan.js computes exact prayer times once, results are memoized, and the compass reuses the last sensor reading instead of recalculating from scratch.
